## Behavior and limits

An exact saved intent survives interruption by design; different form terms stop
before another approval/dispatch. The error now offers the connected owner
**Review saved request**, performing the existing guarded read. Recovery is inside
the fixed-header spacing and receives explicit scroll/focus. Healthy/unrelated
errors get no extra control. Original terms remain visible; Restore is review,
Continue stays separate, Cancel requires confirmation and backend fencing.
Submitted/unknown transfer and failed-read states remain protected/truthful.
No automatic cancellation, clearing, approval, account adoption or replay.

The customer's original interruption cause, current phase and transfer outcome
remain unknown. Prior successful customer borrowing is historical evidence,
not proof this later request is recovered. SEC12 stays open until live software
and actual customer acceptance are established; SEC14's successful owner swap
closure stays separate.
